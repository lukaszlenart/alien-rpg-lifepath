#ALIEN RPG EVOLVED EDITION  

Lifepath Generator  

##Lifepath Generator Overview:

1. Determine your nature (human, android, etc.)
2. Roll for your origin and gain 3 points in random general skills. 
3. Determine your early life, gaining 2 attribute points and 2 skill points.
4. Experience a life event, gaining 2 more attribute points and 2 points in random general skills. 
5. Gain 2 points in attributes of your choice and then apply for your first career. If successful, you gain 2 points in random career skills, a roll for gear, and a roll for your first paycheck. 
6. Serve your first term – roll to see where you were posted and how well you performed. You gain 1 more point in a random career skill, a random talent, another roll for gear, and another paycheck roll. 
7. Did you want to serve a second and third term? Follow the same process for your first term, but for each extra term you must reduce an attribute of your choice by 1 point. 
8. Get your psych evaluation. 9\. The final touches – your PC’s name, appearance, age, personal agenda, signature item, buddy and rival.

## DETERMINE YOUR NATURE.  
Roll 2D6. On a result of 11 or 12, you are an android. If you are an android, decide with your GM if you are a secret android (page 25). Then roll a D66. On a 66, you are an A.W. (Artificial Womb) Soldier (if human) or a hybrid (if android). Discuss with your GM if you wish to use the alternate Kid rules at this point.

Humans start with a base score of 2 in each attribute. Androids and hybrids may add \+3 to two attributes of their choice before starting their life path, and A.W. Soldiers may add \+1 or \+2 to their Strength by deducting from their Empathy. By the end of the life path, a human must have a score of at least 2 in every attribute and cannot have any score greater than 5, while androids and hybrids can finish with attribute scores up to 8, and A.W. Soldiers may have a Strength up to 7 with an Empathy score no higher than 3 (minimum 1). Skill levels can never exceed 5 for any PC. Re-roll or choose another result if the life path generates otherwise.

## ORIGIN  
Roll 2D6 on the Origin table to find out the super-nation of origin, then D6 twice for your sub-affiliation and home. Finally, gain 3 points in random general skills (page 286). For A.W. Soldiers, your supernation is United Americas with UAAC/W-Y sub-affiliation. Roll for UA Home, with 1 point in each Marine skill.

## EARLY LIFE  
What was your early life like, who did you share it with, and what experiences did you have? Roll D66 three times on the Early Life table, once for each column, and add the specified attribute and skill points (2 of each) to your scores. Decide the precise details of your early life experience, depending on your nature from Step 1\. You may also gain modifiers to your psych evaluation roll – note these as you go. For hybrids, keep in mind that their early life will have been as a child. For A.W. Soldiers, use the alternate A.W. Soldier Early Life table.

## LIFE EVENT  
Your life event can take place at any point in the lifepath of your choosing, but is decided now by a D66 roll on the Life Event table. These events offer story hooks and may leave a lasting impact on your PC – the impact should be roleplayed. Agree to the specific details of any event with your GM. Your life event gives you 2 specified attribute points and 2 points in random general skills (page 286). For hybrids, keep in mind that their early life will have been as a child. For A.W. Soldiers, use the alternate A.W. Soldier  Life Event table.

### SPECIAL NOTE FOR HYBRIDS  
All Hybrids also have another special life event where your PC’s former life as a child has ended and their new life in a synthetic body begins. Determine the narrative details between you and your GM \-- a common theme involves a secret corporate project with a terminally-ill child using proprietary technologies and information stolen from Prodigy Corporation.

## CAREER APPLICATION  
Next, gain 2 points in attributes of your choice and then apply for your first career. The Careers & Career Skills table lists the minimum attribute scores for each career, their paychecks, and key skills. To apply, make a skill roll against one of the skills listed for the career. The roll cannot be pushed.

*  If unsuccessful, you are rejected and must apply to a different career (however, you may pull a fast one, see “PULL A FAST ONE”).  
*  If you roll one 6 you join as a standard recruit.  
*  If you roll two or more 6s someone has noted your potential, and you gain a favor – make a roll on the Favor table.

NOTE: There are special considerations for if you are applying for a MOS, as an A.W. Soldier, as a Kid, as a Lowlife, or as Android or Hybrid, as well as more information to “PULL A FAST ONE” for a failed roll. If one or more of those applies for your lifepath, please read the relevant information for potential considerations and adjustments to Career Application.

### APPLYING FOR A MOS (MILITARY OCCUPATION SPECIALTY)  
The following must be done to apply for a MOS:  
1\. You must apply as a Colonial Marine with at least a STR 3 (not 4\) score, have more than one success (or pull a fast one) and forfeit your favor to then apply to a MOS. Each Marine skill must have at least 1 skill point allocated as well.  
2\. To apply, make a skill roll against one of the skills listed for the MOS. The roll cannot be pushed.

* If unsuccessful, you are rejected and must either apply for a different career (if STR is less than 4), settle for no MOS (if STR is at least 4\) or apply to a different MOS (however, you may pull a fast one). NOTE: If you pulled a fast one in step 1 but apply for a different career after, the consequences of a fast one still apply \-- talk to your GM about how that might manifest.  
* If you roll one 6 you join as a standard recruit for that MOS.  
* If you roll two or more 6s someone has noted your potential, and you gain a favor – make a roll on the table on the next page.

### APPLYING AS AN A.W. SOLDIER  
If your PC is an A.W. Soldier, you must apply as a Colonial Marine as well as for a MOS. A failed roll in either stage means someone above you automatically pulled a fast one on your behalf. See “PULL A FAST ONE” for more information.

#### PULL A FAST ONE  
If you fail an application, you can pull a fast one. Your PC does something underhanded or illegal to get around the system, or begs a favor from someone in the hierarchy – settle the details with the GM. Your previously unsuccessful roll now counts as one success, and you’re in\! But a dangerous secret now hangs over you, or you owe someone a big favor.

### APPLYING A KID OR LOWLIFE  
The Kid and Lowlife are not careers, they are lifestyles. You don’t need to apply to play a Kid or Lowlife, you may just choose these careers. However, Kids are limited to only one term for their career, while the other careers (including Lowlife) can be extended up to three terms. This applies whether using rules as written or the alternate rules for Kids.

### APPLYING AS A HYBRID OR ANDROID  
While their former life was as a child, a hybrid may select any career they are qualified for, using all the same rules as normal. Whether as a hybrid or an android, certain career options such as Kid may raise roleplaying questions best discussed with your GM during character generation.

## YOU’RE HIRED!
Once recruited, you go into basic training, get geared up, and get your first paycheck.

* Roll D3 twice on the table below for career skills, gaining 1 skill level for each roll. If you applied for a MOS, roll a d6 twice for the 5 skills available (1-3 as Colonial marine, 4-5 from MOS). On a 6, reroll or choose one of the career/MOS skills yourself.  
* Roll once on the gear table for your career. Re-roll if you roll an item you already have. If you selected a MOS, you may opt for the loadout specific for that MOS.  
* Roll for your first paycheck. Any MOS uses the Colonial Marine paycheck.

## CAREER TERMS  
You must serve at least one term in your career but may choose to serve up to three (with the exception of the Kid career, which can only serve one). Follow these steps for each term (for first term MOS or Military Officer/Pilot, see note):

1. Posting: What did you do during this term? Roll D6 on the Career Posting table found with your career information to find out. This can give you a bonus gear roll, and you also gain 1 point in a random career skill.
2. Performance: How well did you do your job? Make a skill test against a career skill of your choice (you cannot push):
  * If you fail, you suffer a disaster (roll on the table above) and get –1 on your psych evaluation roll (page 288).  
  * If you roll one 6 your performance has been satisfactory.  
  * If you roll two or more 6s you have performed well and gain a favor (roll on the table above) and get \+1 on your psych evaluation roll (page 288). Re-reroll any favors you already have.    
3. Talent: Roll a D6 on the Career Talent table. For your second and third term, you may roll or choose from the Random General Talents table.  
4. Gear & Paycheck: Make one roll for career gear. Re-roll any items you already have and make a paycheck roll. If you selected a MOS, you may opt for the loadout specific for that MOS if not already done so.

### FOR THOSE WITH A MOS OR MILITARY OFFICERS AND PILOTS  
If you selected a MOS, an officer or pilot for the Marines, you may substitute one term with the CMOM Field Events. If you are a normal enlisted marine or a military pilot/crew chief, you may only substitute your first term using MOS CAREER TERM A \- ENLISTED and MOS CAREER TERM B \- PILOTS AND CREW CHIEFS. For a military officer or a NCO/unit commander (any MOS), you may substitute your first term with CAREER TERM A \- ENLISTED and a second term with the MOS CAREER TERM C \- OFFICER.

## PSYCH EVALUATION  
Each character ends their life path with their psych evaluation, so the company can see what they’ve got on their hands. Androids undergo a “product cognitive stability test,” and also roll on the table. Players should roleplay the results. Roll 2D6 on the table on page 288 and apply any modifiers you gained during your life path.

## FINAL TOUCHES  
Roll, select, or make up your character’s name, appearance, age, personal agenda, and signature item (unless they are an android). Consider who their  buddies and rivals may be as well. To complete this, keep in mind that your PC’s formative and life events may drive their personal agenda. Good luck out there – you’re gonna need it.

### NAMING A.W. SOLDIERS AND HYBRIDS  
For A.W. Soldiers, in order to avoid any unintentional mix-up among the ranks, an artificial womb soldier is always marked with the middle initials A.W. and has the surname of a weapon such as Sword, Arrow, or Hatchet.

For Hyrbids, it’s common for them to have a “code name” they go by, often imposed by the corporation sponsoring the hybrid transition and discouraged from using their former human name. The new name can be anything you and the GM wish, and may include references to literary works, famous figures, or the name of the synthetic model used, Using their human name may help a Hybrid PC hold onto their humanity or may cause unwanted stress depending on how they feel about the transition.

### SIGNATURE ITEMS FOR HYBRIDS  
Hybrids are afforded more flexibility for what they can determine is their signature item as it plays a more critical role as their connection to their former humanity. For more information, read the HOMEBREW RULES: HYBRIDS section.

### OPTIONAL FRONTIER PERSONAL AGENDA  
For Personal Agendas, you may roll on your career’s Personal Agenda table or on the Frontier Personal Agendas table. The former are tailored to the career, while the latter are tailored towards goals specific to the PC setting out far from civilization. Rolling for both could help inspire a unique one of your own\!

### OPTIONAL: PLAYING AS A CYBORG  
If, after making a human PC, you wish for them to be a cyborg, discuss with your GM regarding what that might mean for your character. What augmentations might the cybernetics provide for the PC? What corporation do they owe their life for the augmentations? While no homebrew rules are presented, a suggested rule of thumb is that any use of the augmentation cannot benefit from successes of stress dice. The only limitation is that the transfer of a human mind into a synthetic body would be considered a Hyrbid with its own rules.

### HOMEBREW RULES: KID CHARACTERS  
Kids can have any “career” and suggestions are provided as to what it might mean for a kid to have that role. Your GM may also bar certain talents at their discretion.

Below are example Kid variations of careers:
* Colonial Marine: A military brat or a bully  
* Colonial Marshall: A junior reporter or kid detective  
* Company Agent: A spoiled brat or a rich kid  
* Lowlife: A delinquent or an outcast  
* Medic: A nurse-in-training or a dreamer  
* Officer: A model student or a popular kid  
* Pilot: A bicycle/tricycle rider or a wild kid  
* Roughneck: A tinkerer or a jock  
* Scientist: A gifted student or a nerd  
* Wildcatter: A boy/girl scout or outdoorsy kid  
* Entertainer: A class clown or drama kid

Rules-wise, kids also work a little differently from standard characters:
* Kids start with only 10 points for their starting attribute points, with each attribute ranging from 1 to 3, though they may place a 4 in their career’s key attribute.  
* Kids are adaptable with their future ahead of them, and so do not need to allocate 2 of their skill points until the skill is first used in gameplay. Normal limits for how points are allocated are otherwise followed from character creation.  
* Kids are notoriously stubborn. In addition to the usual rules for pushing rolls, a kid may also push a roll once more beyond.  
* Kids recover from stress more easily with their signature item. When a kid uses their signature item, they reduce their stress by two steps instead of one.  
* Kids shape the stories that involve them and learn quickly. If a Kid PC would earn a Story Point, they instead earn 2 (with a maximum of 6). In Campaign play, a Story Point may be obtained for a Kid PC for every 5XP spent (max 6\)

Discuss with your GM what personal agendas, appearance, starting gear and cash is appropriate for your kid (in general, cash is an order of magnitude less than their adult counterparts). The GM is encouraged to consider the original Kid career qualities in conjunction with the career of choice (if different from Lowlife) when deciding.  

### HOMEBREW RULES: HYBRIDS

Hybrid PCs play the same as android PCs with a few exceptions:

* Not only can a Hybrid opt to keep their synthetic nature a secret, they can keep the fact that they are a Hybrid secret as well \-- something that might be imposed upon their corporate guardians. Revealing their true nature as a Hybrid incurs the same increase in stress and triggering a stress response as with revealing their nature as a synthetic, and revealing both inflict 2 stress and 2 stress responses.   
* A Hybrid can push rolls, gain stress, suffer stress and panic responses, and incur mental trauma just as humans can, though do not suffer stress specifically from fatigue.  
* Stress cannot be reduced by most means other than a Comtech roll and a Shift of work alongside using their Signature Item to reduce any incurred stress back to zero. Healing mental trauma also requires a Comtech roll and use of their Signature Item over a period of a week in addition to the Empathy roll.  
* A Hybrid’s signature item is even more critical to their stress management than for humans, serving as a link to their former human life. Their Signature Item can still be used to reduce their Stress by 1 per session as well as talents relying on their Signature Item, such as Hold Your Nerve. In addition, with GM permission, their signature item may be an NPC close to them or larger item critical to their humanity. Permanent loss of the hybrid’s signature item will likely sever their connection to humanity \-- the PC reverts to fully synthetic rules, and should be roleplayed accordingly.  
* A hybrid character may spend 5 XP to gain a talent uniquely crafted to them. Discuss with your GM about what that talent could be, informed by the narrative the PC has experienced, such as having the Remote talent without needing the proper gear and apply to non-remote comtech, or having the Husbandry talent apply to a particular Xenomorph not normally allowed. A PC may obtain more than one of these unique talents, and the GM has the final call about what is allowed. At minimum, a hybrid PC can gain a story point by spending 5 XP.

The alternate Kid rules can be applied to a Hybrid PC even if the Hybrid PC has not selected Kid as their career. Work with your GM to discuss how the two homebrew rulesets will work together for your play experience.

## CLASS: LOWLIFE (homebrew replacement for Kid)

It’s criminal how many aren’t paid enough to even live, and yet you’re one of those many considered the scum of society, regardless of if you’ve ever broken the law or not. You do what you have to do to get by though--squatting on worlds no angels dare tread, taking jobs everyone else is too good for, ready to run when trouble rises so you can live another day. No matter what others say, you know you always pull through for those close to you, and you always stay true to your own code, ever faithful to what you believe in above all else.
